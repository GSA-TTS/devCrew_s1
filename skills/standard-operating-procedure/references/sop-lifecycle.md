# SOP review, approval, training and review interval

Use these stages for the lifecycle block that follows the SOP. Name each role that owns a stage; never a person.

| Stage | What happens | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Review | Technical review for accuracy and completeness. Quality or compliance review for regulatory alignment and template adherence. Optional legal review for high-risk processes (safety, data protection, employment, contracts). | Subject-matter expert; quality or compliance; optionally legal when high-risk |
| Approval | Final sign-off by the accountable manager and the quality or governance function, with recorded signatures or e-signatures. | Accountable manager; quality or governance |
| Training and competence | Training matched to risk and complexity: briefing, hands-on practice, or e-learning with assessment. Keep attendance and comprehension records as compliance evidence. | Owner |
| Periodic review and retirement | Review annually for high-risk SOPs and every 2 to 3 years for lower-risk ones, with the owner named. Retire or merge obsolete SOPs. | Owner |

## Test before release

Have someone who was not involved in the design run the task from the draft alone. Every point where they stop to ask is a step to rewrite.
