# Measures, coverage and quality dimensions

## 1. Length and readability rows

Columns: Measure, Original, Condensed, Change.

- Words: count of words.
- Sentences: count of sentences; count each list item or numbered step as one sentence.
- Average words per sentence: words divided by sentences.
- Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level: 0.39 x (words / sentences) + 11.8 x (syllables / words) - 15.59. It gives the U.S. school grade needed to understand the text; lower is easier.
- Flesch Reading Ease: 206.835 - 1.015 x (words / sentences) - 84.6 x (syllables / words). Scale 0 to 100; higher is easier.
- Compression ratio: (1 - condensed words / original words) x 100, in percent. Change for this row reads a dash.

What the formulas do not measure: logical organization, use of examples, layout, the reader's prior knowledge, concept difficulty and sentence complexity beyond length. Use them with the comprehension review, and do not trade clarity for a better score.

## 2. Coverage rows

Columns: Element, Original, Condensed, Criterion, Met.

| Element | Criterion |
|---|---|
| Core concepts | 100 percent carried over |
| Key-term definitions | 100 percent carried over |
| Prerequisites | 100 percent carried over |
| Essential procedure steps | 100 percent carried over |
| Important procedure steps | at least 90 percent carried over |
| Worked examples | same number or more |

- Original: the number of units of that element in the original.
- Condensed: the number of those units still present in the condensed text, in full or merged into another unit; consolidated sub-steps still count as present.
- Met: met or not met against the criterion. A row whose Original is 0 reads 0, 0 and met.

## 3. Quality dimensions

Columns: Dimension, Agent check, Result, Human review.

| Dimension | Meaning | Agent check | Human review it needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Accuracy | factual correctness of all technical information | every fact, value, procedure step and specification compared with the original | subject matter expert verification of claims, procedures and specifications |
| Completeness | coverage of everything the reader needs | every Coverage row met | subject matter expert side-by-side comparison |
| Clarity | understandable and unambiguous | readability maintained or improved, and no red flag left open | comprehension testing with representative users |
| Consistency | uniform terminology, style and formatting | one term per concept and one format per element throughout | editorial review |
| Correctness | grammar and syntax | condensed text proofread | editorial review |
| Usability | easy to find, understand and apply | every section labelled, every procedure's steps numbered and in correct order | usability testing with representative users |

- Result: met or not met for the agent check.
- Human review: not run, naming the reviewer it needs, unless the user reports that review; then the user's reported outcome.
