# Selection and comparison criteria

Two weighted matrices use these criteria: the Screening matrix (all candidates) and the Comparison matrix (the shortlist, after mapping, readiness and gaps).

## Screening matrix criteria and weight ranges

Choose one weight inside each range so that the six weights sum to 100%, and state the weights and the reason for them with the matrix.

| Criterion | Weight range | What to score |
|---|---|---|
| Strategic alignment | 25-30% | Fit with mission and business objectives; compatibility with culture and operating model; stakeholder support and executive sponsorship |
| Technical feasibility | 20-25% | Complexity and implementation difficulty; compatibility with existing infrastructure and systems; tools and automation available; technical expertise needed and available |
| Resource requirements | 15-20% | Money (licensing, tools, consultants, training); staff time and skill gaps; timeline feasibility; opportunity cost and competing priorities |
| Regulatory and market requirements | 15-20% | Mandatory compliance obligations; customer or partner contract requirements; market expectations; adoption by peers |
| Scalability and adaptability | 10-15% | Maturity and stability of the framework; room for tailoring; scaling across units; update frequency and maintenance burden |
| Risk and impact | 10-15% | Risk-reduction value; consequences of not adopting; implementation risk; change-management complexity |

Priority bands on the weighted total: High above 80%, Medium 60-80%, Low below 60%.

## Comparison matrix dimensions and weights

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---|
| Strategic alignment | 25% |
| Regulatory coverage | 20% |
| Implementation effort | 15% |
| Resource requirements | 15% |
| Stakeholder acceptance | 10% |
| Technical feasibility | 10% |
| Total cost of ownership (3-year implementation and operation) | 5% |

Each rationale names the finding of the earlier parts (mapping, readiness, gap register, barriers, fit findings) that the score rests on.

## Sensitivity

Re-rank the candidates under at least one different weight distribution and say whether the first-ranked framework changes.
