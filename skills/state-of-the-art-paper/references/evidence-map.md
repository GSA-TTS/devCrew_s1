## Column rules

- C-ID: C-, the section or subsection number, then a running number.
- Claim: one sentence, one fact, worded as it will appear in the paper. A draft sentence that needs two facts gets two rows.
- Ref key: the key of the cited document (S- and four digits), used in the draft as [S-0003] until assembly.
- Verbatim quote: at most 40 words, copied from the source text, not from memory and not from the inventory's paraphrase; the inventory quote is only a pointer to it. The Locator gives the section, page or line where the quote is found.
- Status and Tier: the source's status label and tier, both of which travel into the prose.
- Contested?: N, or the C-ID of the counterpart claim; both sides must appear in the same paragraph.
- Use: Q quoted in the text, P paraphrased, N a number is reproduced. An N row also records the denominator or population in its quote.
- Verified: the date and the method of the check.
- A row with Status n.a. or Source date n.a. cannot support a positive factual claim; it supports only a sentence of the form: no fetched primary source establishes ...
